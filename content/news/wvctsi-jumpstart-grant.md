The BMN Lab has received a **WVCTSI JumpStart Grant** from the West Virginia Clinical and Translational Science Institute to support our work on **accelerating Alzheimer's disease biomarker discovery through precision white matter tractometry**.

**What the project is about.** Alzheimer's disease affects the brain's white matter long before symptoms become severe, but standard imaging measures are often too coarse to detect these early changes. Tractometry analyzes diffusion MRI *along* the length of individual white matter bundles, revealing where within a tract microstructure is altered rather than averaging over the whole tract.

We are grateful to WVCTSI for supporting this work.
